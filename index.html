02Research

Feb 2025 — Sep 2025 University of Naples Federico II
Naples

AI Safety Research Engineer

CausalTox — a causal, closed-loop framework for LLM toxicity testing, and the subject of my MSc thesis.

  • Used causal structure learning (DAGMA) to find which prompt-level linguistic factors sit causally upstream of toxic model output, rather than merely correlating with it.
  • Designed and evaluated three red-teaming strategies — single-pass rewriting, a closed feedback loop, and category-specific targeting — against published baselines AutoDAN and GPTFuzz across four victim models.
  • The closed-loop variant, built on an EvoTox-style (1+λ) evolution strategy, outperformed every baseline on 3 of 4 models.
  • Validated all five research questions with paired statistical tests (Wilcoxon signed-rank, rank-biserial effect size), scored via the Perspective API.
